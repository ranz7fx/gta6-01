Dario “Rio” Cruz came back to Solmar with $250 and a borrowed jacket. The city runs on hot cars, hotter tempers and jobs nobody else will take. Drive the avenues, dodge the Solmar PD, run five jobs for Mama Rosa, and turn that $250 into a legend.
